## 🔎 Scope and Verification

- **Agent & LLM Self-Evolution:** self-evolving, self-improving, recursive self-improvement, self-refinement, self-feedback reinforcement, and co-evolving agents or language models
- **LLM Continual Training:** continual pre/post-training, lifelong and online adaptation, retention, and catastrophic-forgetting mitigation
- **Long-Horizon Task Agents:** language agents executing extended, multi-step tasks
- **Excluded:** Agent Memory papers (including cross-topic papers) and journal articles
- **Date boundary:** first publication on or after 2025-05-01; a recent revision does not make an older paper eligible
- **Reinforcement Learning:** substantive RL training or research directly related to language models / language agents, including RLHF, RLAIF, RLVR and GRPO; generic control/robotics RL and incidental mentions are excluded
- **Public inclusion:** any of the four topics or an explicit user request; industry, venue, and citation signals highlight papers without excluding new preprints
- **Acceptance dates:** first observed means newly detected by this bot; it does not establish the actual date of acceptance
- **Conference papers:** only Full/Regular main-track papers qualify by venue; Findings, workshop, demo, and short papers do not
- **Code stars:** displayed as context but never qualify a paper by themselves
- **Industry / venue:** marked only when supported by publication metadata, the paper, a publisher, or an official project page
- Missing metrics are shown as `n/a`, never guessed. Stars and citations are time-sensitive snapshots.
