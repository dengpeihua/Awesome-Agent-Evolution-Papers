# Agent Evolution Curated Corpus Audit

## Scope

The public corpus covers five directions plus explicit requests: papers address Agent/LLM self-evolution, self-improving systems, recursive self-improvement, Agent Memory, LLM continual training, language-model/agent Reinforcement Learning, or long-horizon agents. First publication must be on or after 2025-01-01. Priority evidence is optional.

## Current result

- Topic candidates evaluated: **4505**
- Papers retained in the public repository: **3176**
- CCF-A (Artificial Intelligence): **135**
- Selected top venues: **54**
- Big-tech affiliation: **12**
- At least 10 citations: **197**

Counts overlap because one paper may satisfy multiple qualifiers. GitHub stars are displayed as context but do not qualify a paper for inclusion.

## Evidence boundaries

- Venue qualification uses CCF-A venues in the Artificial Intelligence category, plus the selected venues in docs/curation.md.
- Only main-track Full/Regular conference papers qualify by venue; Findings, workshop, demo, short, submitted, and under-review records do not.
- Big-tech status requires paper-specific affiliation evidence or a first-author industry profile; a generic coauthor profile is insufficient.
- Topical preprints are included without an industry or citation requirement.
- Missing evidence is never inferred.
